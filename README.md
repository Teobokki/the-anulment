# THE ANULMENT

An original story game set at night in the fictional country of **Valdora**. You play **MIYOYAZU**, an ordinary blogger in his mid-20s,
on the night the Constitutional Court annuls the first round of the presidential election and charges the winner, **CLN**.

All characters, parties, countries and events in this game are fictional.

## v1.0.0.1 (Chapter 1: The Annulled Vote + endless free roam)
- Clear night-time Valdora city, slightly damp streets, letterbox cutscenes (no glow / bloom)
- Peaceful protest at the Constitutional Court with placards, candles and the live chant "CLN PREȘEDINTE! / TURUL DOI ÎNAPOI!"
- Poliția Valdorei: shield line, patrols, questioning at restricted areas; pressure meter (warning + respawn, no violence)
- Traffic with fictional brands; **F** takes any vehicle non-violently; police pursue and box you in
- **T** (or CHAT) - type English, MIYOYAZU says it with the offline Romanian Piper voice (bubble + subtitle, history, Esc cancels)
- **EX IX G10** phone (P): CAMERA with SELMOTES + TAKE PHOTO, MESSAGES, NEWS, MAP, EVIDENCE, RECORDER, SETTINGS
- Evidence: photograph/recover clues, interview the prosecutors' aide, evidence board (B) - PUBLISH THE TRUTH (news + crowd reaction, no ending)
- Endless free roam: new tips to photograph and post, random protest rallies, live news feed; credits only from the menu
- Minimap + full map (M), save/load, pause, settings (master/music/effects/voice volume, subtitles)
- Per-user install, no admin; built-in updater (latest.json)
